# FAQ

## What does PacBao do?

It turns your website into an app-style entry with its own name and icon. After install, users open your site like an app — without building a native product from scratch.

## What does the result feel like?

Users tap an icon and land on your website. The experience is web-based, with your branding, so it works well as a product or campaign entry.

## Do I need native coding skills?

No. Prepare a URL, app name, and optional icon, then follow the UI to export.

## Which platforms are supported?

Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and more. See the product UI for what is available today, and [Platforms](./platforms) for an overview.

## Can I publish to app stores?

You can, but store accounts, review, and compliance are your responsibility. PacBao focuses on fast packaging, trials, and internal distribution.

## Can I package a local folder as an offline app?

Today the product is built around **online URLs**, not a full offline static-site packager.

## Phone app or desktop app?

- Want a quick Android trial → use the phone app  
- Managing multiple projects or exporting several platforms → use desktop  

Both share the same product idea; pick what fits your workflow.

## What if packing or install fails?

Check that the URL is reachable, name/icon are set, and the device allows installs from unknown sources. If it still fails, try another device or contact support.
