# Introduction

**PacBao** turns websites into installable multi-platform apps. Provide a URL, app name, and optional icon, then export mobile or desktop packages so users can open your site from a dedicated icon.

## What you can do

Many products only need a dedicated entry to a web page — not a full native app. PacBao reduces that to:

1. Enter URL and app details  
2. Choose target platforms  
3. Export, install, or distribute  

Great for independent sites, campaign pages, admin portals, and lightweight SaaS front ends.

## What you get

| Value | Why it matters |
|-------|----------------|
| Faster launch | No need to build a native wrapper from scratch |
| Multi-platform reach | Export Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and more from one project |
| Branded entry | Custom name and icon so users recognize your product |
| Flexible workflows | Quick Android trials on phone; project management and multi-platform export on desktop |

## Who it is for

- Operators and indie developers who want an app-style entry for a site  
- Small teams that need lightweight delivery and fast installers  
- Creators who want the same content on multiple platforms  

## Boundaries

- Best for apps whose content is an **online URL**  
- Focused on trials, sideload, and internal distribution; store submission remains yours  
- Not a replacement for full native product development (deep system APIs, complex native features, etc.)
